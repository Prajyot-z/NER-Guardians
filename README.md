# BhuRakshak

### AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring System for the North Eastern Region

**Predict. Explain. Map. Warn. Prioritise.**

---

## 🌍 Overview

**BhuRakshak** is an AI-powered disaster-management platform designed to help monitor and predict **landslide risk across the North Eastern Region (NER) of India**.

It combines **AI/ML, GIS, rainfall and weather data, terrain information, satellite intelligence, historical landslide records, and field observations** to transform fragmented data into actionable risk intelligence.

> **Where is the risk? When could it increase? Why is it increasing? What could be affected?**

---

## 🚨 Problem

The North Eastern Region is highly vulnerable to landslides due to **heavy rainfall, steep and fragile terrain, slope instability, flash floods, and unplanned hill cutting**.

Conventional monitoring can be reactive and fragmented, making it difficult to identify emerging risks early and prioritise areas requiring immediate attention.

---

## 💡 Solution

BhuRakshak provides a unified platform for:

* **Landslide risk prediction**
* **Real-time risk visualisation through GIS**
* **Weather and rainfall monitoring**
* **Satellite-based environmental monitoring**
* **Field and citizen reporting**
* **Impact assessment**
* **Early warnings and alerts**
* **Emergency response prioritisation**

---

## 🧠 Risk Intelligence

BhuRakshak analyses landslide risk through three levels:

**Susceptibility → Hazard → Risk**

* **Susceptibility:** Where are landslides more likely based on terrain and historical factors?
* **Dynamic Hazard:** How are current and forecast conditions increasing near-term likelihood?
* **Risk:** What could be affected if the hazard increases?

The system can generate a **0–100 risk score**, risk severity, confidence level, and forecast trends.

---

## 🤖 AI + GIS

The AI/ML layer analyses factors such as:

* Rainfall and cumulative rainfall
* Soil moisture
* Slope and elevation
* Land cover and terrain characteristics
* Historical landslide activity
* Environmental changes

Machine-learning models can be used to estimate landslide likelihood and provide **explainable risk factors**.

GIS then converts these predictions into interactive spatial intelligence, showing:

**Risk Zones • Roads • Villages • Infrastructure • Historical Landslides • Rainfall • Field Reports**
